Privacy

How your data is handled.

Here is how the current app is designed to handle data. These notes will be updated as we prepare the app for release.

On your phone and in the cloud

Saved chats, files and settings are stored on your device. For a cloud reply, your message, recent chat context and any attached photos pass through our Supabase service to the AI provider. Cloud replies are processed online, rather than only on your phone.

The current free Gemini service may use messages and replies to improve its products, including review by people. Please keep personal, sensitive and confidential information out of this service. App access will stay closed while we review a suitable way to handle this data.

Connected accounts and backups

Connecting a Google account is optional. In the current design, connector tokens and mail processing stay on your device, separate from cloud chat. If you choose chat backup, it is saved in your own Google Drive app-data folder.

We are reviewing account permissions so they match the features offered. This website does not connect to your accounts or ask for those permissions.

Delete your account and data

We are building the account-deletion flow. It is not available yet, and signing out does not delete an account. The flow needs to cover local files, server records, shared-learning data and optional Drive backups.

Before app access opens, we need a verified way to request deletion in the app and on the website, with contact details and clear information about retention and any exceptions. This preview cannot receive a deletion request yet.

This preview website

This website has no added analytics or advertising, and it does not accept account signups or form submissions. The age preview is not saved. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, even when visitors are not signed in. You can read the hosting details and GitHub privacy statement.

Safety and availability

AI is a helpful starting point, but it can make mistakes. For important medical, legal, financial or technical decisions, please check with a qualified source. We have not yet verified how the app performs with a large number of active users.

We are also preparing a way to report unsafe answers, support and privacy contact details, and the remaining service and reliability checks. These notes explain our progress; they are not a legal certification or a promise of zero risk.
